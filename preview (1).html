Anything else?

🧩 Café Detective — comprehension

1. What can the assistant say first?
2. How can a customer ask about the price?
3. Which phrase checks whether the customer will eat at the café?
4. Which question is about a drink's temperature?

🎭 Café Challenge — pair role-play

Use at least 4 useful phrases. Swap roles and repeat.

👤 Student A — Customer

  • Order a hot chicken sandwich.
  • Ask for water and tea.
  • Ask how much it is.
  • Ask if you can pay by card.

🧑‍🍳 Student B — Assistant

  • Ask how the customer wants to pay.
  • Ask small or big coffee.
  • Ask eat in or take out.
  • Ask hot or cold.
